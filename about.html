About TopCoir

A collaboration between
Cocowide Lanka & Ceycoir

Two family-built Sri Lankan manufacturers, one shared belief: that the world's growers deserve coir they never have to worry about.

Our Story

Built on incremental growth, not internal competition

TopCoir was created so two respected coir producers could reach new customers and new markets together — without diluting the businesses and brands they have each spent years building.

The venture owns a new international brand, develops its own catalogue, and leads the marketing; orders are fulfilled by the partners. Customers buy one consistent product line — TopCoir — backed by the combined capacity and expertise of both manufacturers.

Stronger Together

Synergy of Two Leading Players

Combining effort of two industry leaders creates a unified powerhouse in Sri Lanka's eco-friendly coco-substrate industry by merging their strengths and integrating years of manufacturing experiences of coir-based substrates which ultimately enables Topcoir serving everyone from home gardeners to large commercial hydroponic farms.

Furthermore, this synergy guarantees strict regulatory compliance for global markets through pooled certifications like ISO 9001, ISO 14001, HortiCert, OMRI, and GMP, while enabling mixed-container load optimization and stabilized, year-round inventory across North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia.

Premium Sri Lankan coir solutions. A joint venture by Cocowide Lanka and Ceycoir, bringing two trusted manufacturers together under one international brand.
