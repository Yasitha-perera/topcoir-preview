Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you share with us.

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

When you submit an enquiry or newsletter form, we collect the details you provide — such as your name, company, email, country, product interest and message. We also record the date and your IP address for security and spam prevention.

How we use it

We use your information solely to respond to your enquiry, provide quotes and samples, and — if you opt in — send occasional product news. We do not sell or rent your data to third parties.

Cookies

This site uses only essential cookies required for it to function (for example, to keep the admin dashboard secure). We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

Data retention

Enquiries are retained for as long as needed to serve you and meet our business and legal obligations. You may ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal data we hold about you by emailing hello@topcoir.com.
