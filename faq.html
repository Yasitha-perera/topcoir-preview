Help & Answers

Frequently asked questions

Everything growers and buyers usually ask before they order. Can't find your answer? Get in touch.

Do you supply to my country?+

Yes — TopCoir exports to growers and distributors in 40+ markets worldwide. Tell us your destination and volumes on the contact form and we'll confirm logistics and lead time.

Can I order a sample before committing?+

Absolutely. Sample and trial quantities are available for most products. Use the "Request a sample" option on the contact page and mention the product and crop.

What EC and pH can you supply?+

Our washed, buffered media is typically supplied at EC < 0.5 mS/cm and pH 5.5–6.8. We can formulate to your specification for larger orders.

What is the minimum order quantity?+

MOQs vary by product and packaging — from retail cartons to full container loads. Send us your requirement and we'll advise the most efficient option.

How is the coir packaged for export?+

Products are compressed where possible, palletised and container-loaded to maximise volume efficiency. Retail and bulk packaging are both available.

Is your coir sustainable?+

Yes. Coir is a renewable by-product of the coconut harvest — no tree is grown or felled for coir alone, and every part of the husk is put to use.

Do you offer custom blends?+

We do. The TopCoir signature line includes custom blends made to your EC, pH, particle size and packaging spec. Talk to our team to get started.
