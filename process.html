Step by Step

How coir is made

1 · Raw Material Production

Turning whole coconut husks into the fibre, pith and chips that every product starts from.

Fiber Extraction

Husks are soaked and mechanically de-fibred to separate long coir fibre from the spongy pith.

Husk Chip Production

Husks are cut into graded chips (4S and 7 / 10 / 20 mm) for aeration-focused products.

Husk Crushing

Husk material is crushed to the grade required for clean pith and fibre recovery.

Fiber Cutting / Crushing

Fibre is cut and crushed to short-fibre lengths (0–10 / 0–15 mm) for blending.

Information only

2 · Preparation of Raw Materials

Cleaning, stabilising and grading the material so it performs predictably for sensitive crops.

Washing / Buffering

Pith is washed to lower EC and buffered with calcium nitrate to stabilise it for sensitive crops.

Drying

Material is brought to a controlled target moisture through sun and mechanical drying.

Screening

Screened and graded by particle size so each product line meets a consistent specification.

Information only

3 · Preparation of Final Products

Pressing and forming the prepared media into the finished formats growers order.

Vertical Pressing (Grow Bags)

Media is vertically pressed and packed into grow bags for ready-to-plant formats.

Horizontal Pressing

Horizontal pressing forms blocks, bricks and slabs at product-specific compression ratios.

Information only
